\section{Implementation Status and Evidence}
The repository currently supplies deterministic shelf/house generation, the
canonical MABEL model, native MuJoCo actuator stepping, stocking checks, per-step
contact traces, IK prefiltering, and a conventional tracking module. The physical
script currently uses the left arm and shared lift. A two-arm tracking interface
does not establish coordinated bimanual manipulation.

The first recorded approach failed with 17.4\,cm of requested-hand error and
base tilt. After prefiltering, the diagnostic rejected the initial grasp and
executed a bounded staging prefix instead of closing on an unacceptable pose.
Before gravity compensation, that prefix had about 1.2\,cm of measured-versus-
predicted hand error. These runs motivate separating IK residual, joint/load
tracking, and base displacement. They are failure diagnostics on a single bench,
not a success-rate experiment or evidence of generalization.

Subsequent development trials completed both cartons on seeded local scenes.
Each product passed measured lift, shelf-back contact dwell and release checks;
both products then satisfied the environment's stable placement conditions.
A recorded seed-0 two-carton episode took 87.36 simulated seconds with no
interventions. Its peak hand, back-contact and arm/fixture normal forces were
13.6, 5.4 and 6.1\,N. Both MP4 views contain 874 frames at 10\,fps, including a
labeled final pause. These changing-controller, reused-seed development cases
are not a frozen-policy benchmark or a generalization estimate.

Selected actual outcomes and source paths are retained in
\path{paper/evidence/development_runs.json}. One earlier parallel render process
crashed natively without a terminal summary; it remains unverified. Subsequent
video generation used a single rendering process, and the campaign driver
retains process failures instead of dropping them.

\paragraph{Frozen-policy carton pilot.}
A subsequent controller opens the native left thumb MCP to 0.4\,rad, clearing
the carton's top edge before closure. The posture is represented in both actuator
references and predicted finger geometry, with unchanged native robot geometry,
gains, collision masks and limits. The 25\,N force abort and all task gates remain
fixed. At policy revision \texttt{95107d7}, this controller completes all ten
two-carton scenes in each of two previously used regression suites, improving
their earlier 8/10 and 7/10 results to 10/10 and 10/10. A separate ten-scene suite
generated after policy freeze also completes 10/10 scenes. All 60 scheduled
cartons in the selected-controller cohorts pass measured pick, shelf support,
final positioning and clean retreat gates. A physics-step observer detects no
tip/drop incident; episode logs record zero interventions.
The fresh-suite Wilson 95\% interval is approximately 72.2--100\%.
These small, stationary, left-arm, privileged-state carton pilots do not establish
general shelf solvability or coordinated bimanual behavior. Full configurations,
source fingerprints, stage evidence and rejected development probes are recorded
in \path{docs/reports/thumb_preshape_followup.json} and its accompanying report.

A bounded seven-configuration search on separate development scenes selects
4.5\,N balanced grip and 1.875\,N push targets. The search retains thirteen
verified episode outcomes and one unknown final orchestration outcome; the
unknown candidate is rejected. With the selected settings frozen, a further
ten-scene force-validation suite completes 10/10, versus 8/10 at the earlier
force settings. The same selected settings retain 10/10 on each earlier suite,
giving 40/40 complete scenes and all four stages for 80 scheduled cartons.
Across these forty matched cases, the mean per-episode highest monitored force
decreases from 10.159 to 8.032\,N. Full paired evidence and the unknown search
outcome are retained in \path{docs/reports/compliance_followup.json}.
These shelves have ample lateral room; this pilot does not measure insertion
with only 5--10\,mm total unused width across an occupied shelf row.

The conventional controller added in this draft is evaluated with dedicated
native-physics tracking tests. Numerical summaries and commands belong in the
controller runbook; a test pass establishes only its stated fixture and bounds.
Broader stocking generalization, verified recovery, autonomous navigation, teleoperation quality,
demonstration replay, and learned-controller superiority are not established.
