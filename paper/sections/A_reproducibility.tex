\section{Reproducibility and Claim Boundaries}
Record scene configuration and seed, actual dependency/model revisions,
actuator/transmission settings, controller parameters, software and hardware
versions, timestep, observation period, and the policy/weight hash. Preserve
initial integration state, action references, applied actuator targets,
physics-step measurements, event/rejection records, and terminal outcomes.
Full recordings remain under ignored output directories; commit only small
summaries needed to reproduce a stated claim.

Source modules live under \path{src/abstract_stack/}.
Environment stepping is in \path{env.py}; prediction is in \path{ik.py}.
The tracker is in \path{mabel_controller.py}.
The physical solver is in \path{interactions.py}.
Detailed commands and limitations are in the scripted interaction and MABEL
controller runbooks.
The literature review pins its external source version and separates the
authors' findings from our proposed use. A compiled paper is a research artifact,
not an indication that planned experiments have been completed.
