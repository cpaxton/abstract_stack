\section{Scripted Pilot and Planned Experiments}
\subsection{Observed ten-scene scripted pilot}
A public local benchmark uses ten seeded scenes with two rigid cartons each,
one shelf, a stationary MABEL base command and left-arm manipulation in native
MuJoCo physics. The policy reads world state and uses no learned controller or
language model. Two seeds had appeared in earlier development; this is a pilot
validation suite, not a blind held-out generalization experiment.

The default script completed all four measured stages in six of ten scenes
(95\% Wilson interval 31.3--83.2\%). Verified pick and shelf support each passed
for 16 of 20 scheduled cartons; final positioning and completed retreat each
passed for 14 of 20. Unreached cartons remain in every denominator. Two scenes
aborted at the unchanged 25\,N hand-contact limit during approach; one rejected
the second carton's opening/retreat path before actuation; one aborted during
the supported push at the same force limit. Earlier successful stage gates do
not imply that a product remains stably placed at the end of a failed scene.

A read-only observer samples every product at each 2\,ms physics step after
initial scene settling. It retains any tip beyond 45 degrees or drop below
0.25\,m even if the product subsequently recovers. No such incidents occurred
in these ten baseline episodes. Retreat additionally requires the current and
previously placed cartons to satisfy the unchanged placement stability dwell.
This monitoring measures a specific simulated tip/drop definition, rather than
certifying collision-free manipulation or universal recovery.

A bounded coordinate hill-climb evaluated seven controller configurations on
two separate development seeds: the default, and 0.75/1.25 multipliers for the
squeeze target, push-force target and payload-orientation speed. Selection
prioritized complete scenes, safety/evidence, successful stages and lower
peak contact force before completion time. All six neighbours were rejected:
four lost a complete development scene, and two preserved success but were
slower without a lower peak. The default therefore remains selected; this
search did not establish an improved controller. A tightened scoring audit
that penalizes unknown outcomes and knock-overs left every decision unchanged.

An initial unbounded development probe on a third seed exceeded five minutes
of computation without a terminal summary and was stopped, retaining an
unknown outcome. The bounded search used the two disclosed development seeds;
this exclusion limits its coverage. Subsequent physics campaigns impose a
180\,s wall budget per episode and retain unknown results in the denominator.

\subsection{Contact-control follow-up}
A later scripted revision searches bounded coupled opening/withdrawal alternatives
and uses native wrist-mount/board clearance with measured fixture-force unloading.
It retains the original task criteria, native model and 25\,N abort threshold;
both comparison programs now include non-grasping arm links in active-product
force coverage. The selected program improves the public regression suite from
6/10 to 8/10, retaining every baseline success. Pick and shelf-support gates remain
16/20; final positioning and completed retreat improve from 14/20 to 16/20.
Release search alone reaches 7/10. A guarded-approach prototype regressed to 4/10
on this suite despite 8/10 development outcomes, and remains disabled by default.

After freezing the selected policy at commit \texttt{c60d009}, a fresh paired
ten-scene suite gives 7/10 verified complete scenes versus 4/10 for legacy, with
14/20 versus 10/20 final-positioning and retreat gates. The baseline includes one
SIGSEGV without a terminal summary, counted as unknown with no stage credit.
A separate serial retry succeeded but does not replace that original outcome.
Two other fresh scenes change from verified insertion-force aborts to success;
the crash-to-success pair is not claimed as a known physical recovery.
All twenty scene/model and settled integration-state pairs match, and no verified
baseline success regresses. No tip/drop is observed in verified runs; an unknown
or aborted prefix does not establish post-abort stability.

These are small, same-family studies, not broad generalization evidence. The
selected source, effective flags, full outcomes and limitations are retained in
\path{docs/reports/contact_control_followup.json}. A dual-view recording of the
repaired regression scene contains 928 frames per view at 10\,fps; its applied
controls and final object poses match the headless rollout bitwise.

\subsection{Proposed broader experiments}
\textbf{The following comparisons and larger studies have not been run.}
First replicate the initial physical grasp, lift, contact push and release across
new development instances. Then increase object count and reduce insertion clearance;
introduce prefilled shelves, deliberate jams and tipped products; finally test
both hands and the house layout. Navigation and manipulation results must be
reported separately before claiming whole-house behavior.

\paragraph{Comparisons.}
Use the same measured-state interface, task success checks, physical model,
and matched instance suites for: a hand-written task program; one-shot program
generation; iterative program synthesis with simulator feedback; and, when
available, an explicit TAMP baseline. Cross each supported task program with
the conventional tracker and a compatible learned tracker. An unavailable
baseline is marked not run, not assigned an artificial failure score.

\paragraph{Ablations.}
Measure gravity feedforward, wrist damping, orientation alternatives, collision
prefiltering, object ordering, fallback selection, and contact feedback. Unsafe
ablations still obey force/tilt aborts. Controller and planner changes should
be isolated so an improved grasp controller is not misattributed to task planning.

\paragraph{Development and evaluation.}
Use separate development and validation instances, then freeze code, controller,
model revision, prompts, dependencies, and policy weights before a private test
suite. Propose five independent synthesis runs and 100 paired test episodes per
condition once the physical baseline works. These counts are future targets.
Predeclare budgets, including model usage, simulator transitions, development
time and test-time compute. No paid synthesis or RL campaign is launched by
creating this draft.

Held-out seeds sampled from the same task distribution measure instance
generalization. Separate stress suites change product counts, geometry,
friction, mass, delay, or failure severity and measure distribution shift.
Report them separately. Verify that test seeds and artifacts did not enter
development; preserve failures and timeouts in every denominator.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
The primary outcome is complete-shelf unassisted success, with confidence
intervals and paired comparisons on common instances. Also report partial
completion, simulated completion time, decision compute, total wall time,
interventions, recovery rate and duration, and the number of earlier placements
disturbed. Separate model-synthesis cost, policy computation, and physics time.
Report per-run variation, not only a best policy.

Physical diagnostics include predicted pose residuals, measured Cartesian and
joint tracking, applied target rates, force/impulse by contact category,
base tilt and support, grasp retention, actual lift, and release stability.
Peak force alone is insufficient for contact quality; impulse and sustained
load should accompany it. Complete evaluation requires base-support monitoring
and stronger carried-object collision guarantees than the current finite samples.
