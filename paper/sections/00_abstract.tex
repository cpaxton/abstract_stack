\begin{abstract}
We study a bimanual mobile robot stocking an entire shelf in a continuous
simulation episode. The intended task includes constrained insertion,
contact-guided alignment, rearrangement, and recovery from jams and tipped
products. We propose combining reusable programmatic task policies with
bounded, feedback-controlled manipulation skills. Development-time coding
agents can probe a simulator and revise policies; evaluation freezes the policy,
controller, and physical task definition. Our current MuJoCo implementation
provides MABEL, rigid products, shelf and house scenes, contact measurements,
IK prefiltering, and a conventional bimanual joint/lift tracking foundation.
Initial seeded two-carton episodes complete the physical stocking protocol and
produce synchronized robot-camera and external-view videos. Generalized
performance, bimanual coordination, learned control, and autonomous recovery
remain experimental goals rather than demonstrated results.
\end{abstract}
